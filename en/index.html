Azkar

Free dhikr and digital tasbih counter

Azkar helps you count daily dhikr, keep personal duas, learn the 99 Names of Allah and follow your goals. Install the web app on your phone or use it directly in any modern browser.

Everything for your daily dhikr

  • Simple tasbih counter with custom goals
  • Personal duas and special dhikr lists
  • The 99 Names of Allah
  • Reminders, streaks and useful statistics
  • Offline use with optional Google account synchronization

How to use Azkar

  1. Open Azkar in your browser.
  2. Choose a goal or create your own dhikr.
  3. Tap to count and review your progress.

Frequently asked questions

Is Azkar free?

Yes. Azkar is a free web application and its main features can be used without payment.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. After the first visit, core counting and saved content remain available without an internet connection.

Can I use the same data on another device?

Optional Google account synchronization can transfer supported data between devices. Changes may need time or a connection to appear.
